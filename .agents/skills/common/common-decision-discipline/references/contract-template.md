# Contract Templates

## Quick (tier=low, in chat, no file)

```md
Contract: [one line]
Outcome: ...
Constraints: ...
Non-goals: ...
Acceptance criteria: ...
approval: pending | approved(<who>, <YYYY-MM-DD>) | assumed-autonomous
Reply ok or corrections.
```

## Standard (tier=medium)

```md
# Brief: [Name]
lane: why | direction | both; snc_tier: medium; approval: pending
## Contract
Outcome; Constraints; Non-Goals; Acceptance Criteria
## Why            (Why lane only)
## Options And Recommendation
## Evidence
## Approval
```

## Deep (tier=high)

Standard, plus: decomposition into slices (brainstorm the first slice only), stakeholders, cost-benefit, delivery context, glossary, risk register with owners, and an optional independent review note.
