# Option Card

```md
### Option: [name]
- What: one sentence
- Load-bearing assumption: the fact that must hold
- Evidence limits: what we could not inspect or confirm
- Decisive trade-off: the one trade that should drive the choice
- Proof: the observable result that shows the contract is met
- First failure condition: the first observable sign it is wrong
- Worst plausible case: impact if it fails late
- Cost to abandon: what is thrown away if we switch
```

Lanes:

- Every option shown in a Decision Card or brief includes the `Proof:` line; a single viable path card includes it too.
- Why lane options are business alternatives only: build, buy, defer, do nothing.
- Direction lane options are technical approaches grounded in the evidence ledger.
- One viable path: write one card and state that no real alternative exists.
