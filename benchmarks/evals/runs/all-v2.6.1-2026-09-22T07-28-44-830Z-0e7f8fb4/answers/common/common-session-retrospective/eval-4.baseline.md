- **Candidate skill:** Reject or hold promotion. A held-out failure is evidence the skill needs correction or broader coverage. Reproduce the scenario, identify the missing behavior, update the skill, and rerun the full evaluation.

- **Evaluator:** Treat the rejection as a false negative. Validate the expected answer against the task requirements, then fix or recalibrate the evaluator assertion so it accepts valid answers without broadly weakening correctness checks.

Do not weaken both assertions or promote immediately. Promote only after the candidate passes the corrected evaluator and the evaluator is verified against valid and invalid controls.

Assumption: “assertions” refers separately to candidate-quality checks and evaluator verdict criteria.
