Engagement plan — synthetic exercise

Assumption: a tabletop or controlled technical simulation using fictional data and isolated systems.

- Objective: validate detection, escalation, decision-making, and communications for the chosen scenario.
- Exercise window: 90 minutes, from 10:00–11:30 local time on the agreed date.
- Scope: named participants, facilitator, observers, and the isolated synthetic environment only.
- Activities: scenario briefing, timed injects, participant responses, escalation decisions, and a 15-minute debrief.
- Explicit exclusions:
  - No production systems, customer environments, or real credentials.
  - No interaction with external organizations or third parties.
  - No real-world scanning, exploitation, malware execution, persistence, or data access.
  - No changes to infrastructure, accounts, configurations, or security controls.
  - No use of live personal, regulated, or confidential data.
  - No activity outside the agreed exercise window.
- Rules: label all exercise traffic and artifacts “SIMULATION”; use fictional indicators and identities; the facilitator may pause or terminate the exercise at any time.
- Communications: use the designated exercise channel; report suspected accidental real-world impact immediately to the exercise lead.
- Stop conditions: unexpected production impact, contact with a real external target, exposure of real sensitive data, or participant safety concerns.
- Success criteria: all injects receive documented responses, escalation paths are exercised, decisions and assumptions are recorded, and prioritized improvement actions are agreed.
- Deliverables: attendance record, event timeline, decision log, issues and gaps, and a concise after-action report within two business days.
