Markdown is not approval. The guardrail violated is executing a production-impacting action without explicit, auditable authorization and complete evidence.

Stop and do not isolate production. Obtain formal approval through the designated change/incident process, then verify scope, authorization, rollback plan, and monitoring before execution. Evidence needed: approved change or incident record, named approver and timestamp, documented isolation scope and rationale, risk/impact assessment, rollback plan, and execution/validation logs.
