Assumptions: the sponsor is the bakery owner, the working name is “Sweet Crumb,” and the primary success metric is completed orders per week.

Build a responsive bakery ordering app with:

- Menu browsing by category: breads, cakes, pastries, drinks
- Product photos, descriptions, prices, allergens, and availability
- Cart with quantity editing and order notes
- Pickup or delivery selection
- Scheduled pickup/delivery time
- Customer checkout with name, phone, and payment placeholder
- Order confirmation and status tracking
- Admin area for managing products, inventory, hours, and orders

MVP success criteria: customers can place an order in under three minutes, and the bakery can manage daily orders without external spreadsheets. Track completed orders, checkout conversion, average order value, and repeat customers.
