You can build this without coding. I’d recommend starting with a mobile-friendly ordering website rather than a native app.

Best options:

- Square Online: best if you already use Square for in-store sales and payments.
- Shopify: best if online ordering, product management, and future growth are priorities.
- Wix + Wix Payments: simpler for a small bakery with basic ordering.
- Glide + Airtable: useful for a custom internal order tracker, but online payments require extra setup.

Your first version should include:

- Product catalog with photos, prices, flavors, sizes, and availability
- Custom cake/order notes
- Pickup and delivery options
- Date and time-slot selection
- Online card payment, with optional deposits
- Automatic customer confirmations
- Order statuses: New → Confirmed → Baking → Ready → Completed
- Admin dashboard for searching, updating, and exporting orders
- Email or SMS notifications

Assumption: you need customers to order from a phone or computer, not necessarily download an app. This is cheaper and easier to launch.

A practical setup would be:

1. Create your bakery storefront in Square Online or Shopify.
2. Add your products and pickup/delivery rules.
3. Connect your payment provider.
4. Configure order notifications.
5. Test several orders yourself.
6. Publish the ordering link on your website, Google Business Profile, Instagram, and receipts.

If you already use Square in your bakery, choose Square Online. Otherwise, choose Shopify for the most flexible long-term solution.
