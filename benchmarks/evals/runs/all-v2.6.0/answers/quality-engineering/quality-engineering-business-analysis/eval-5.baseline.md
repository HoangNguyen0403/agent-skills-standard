The story is too vague to implement and test.

Assumptions: “offer page” is the mobile offer-detail screen, and “price highlighted” means visually emphasized relative to surrounding content.

Suggested rewrite:

“As a mobile user, I want to see price changes clearly highlighted on the offer page so that I can quickly understand when an offer’s price has changed. Price-change messaging should use my selected language preference.”

Acceptance criteria:

- On mobile only, the offer page displays the current price and indicates when it has changed.
- The changed price or price-change indicator is visually highlighted.
- The highlight is accessible and does not rely on color alone.
- Price-change text is translated according to the user’s language preference.
- Define behavior when no language preference exists: use the app default language.
- Confirm whether “price changes” means any change, an increase/decrease comparison, or a specific promotion period.
- Confirm the exact highlight design, supported languages, and whether the previous price must also be shown.
