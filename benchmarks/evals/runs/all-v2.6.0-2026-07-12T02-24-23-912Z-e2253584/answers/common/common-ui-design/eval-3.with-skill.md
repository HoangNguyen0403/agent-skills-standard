The problem is not just that the design is simple. It is that the design has no point of view. A white background, soft purple gradient buttons, and generic sans-serif typography are now the default visual shorthand for “AI startup,” so the interface becomes instantly forgettable.

What to change:

1. Choose a real tone and commit to it

- Pick one strong direction such as editorial, luxury, brutalist, retro-futuristic, or organic.
- Do not stay in the safe middle.
- Example: if the product should feel intelligent and authoritative, choose editorial in dark mode rather than “friendly modern SaaS.”

2. Replace generic typography

- Stop using default-feeling UI fonts as the main personality layer.
- Introduce a display font with character for headlines, such as Fraunces, Playfair Display, Cormorant Garamond, or Syne depending on tone.
- Pair it with a restrained body font such as DM Sans, Geist, or IBM Plex Mono.

3. Abandon the white + purple formula

- That palette is overused and rarely connected to the product’s actual story.
- Build a tighter color system with one dominant background, one text color, and one deliberate accent.
- Examples:
  - Ink black + ivory + burnt orange for editorial confidence
  - Deep navy + champagne + muted gold for refined luxury
  - Graphite + electric cyan for a technical control-room feel

4. Make the layout memorable

- Stop centering every block in a predictable stack.
- Use asymmetry, overlap, large type, and intentional negative space.
- Give one section a composition users can recall later, such as an oversized headline colliding with a floating product frame.

5. Improve background and depth

- A plain white canvas leaves the interface with no atmosphere.
- Introduce subtle texture, layered gradients, translucent panels, or fine borders that match the tone.
- Depth should feel designed, not incidental.

6. Simplify motion into one orchestrated entrance

- Do not scatter cute hover effects everywhere.
- Use one clean staggered reveal for the hero or first viewport.
- Keep the motion short, intentional, and easy to disable with `prefers-reduced-motion`.

Concrete redesign direction:

Purpose: Reposition the product as distinctive and credible instead of “another AI tool.”

Tone: Brutally minimal or editorial dark mode.

Differentiation: A dramatic headline system with asymmetrical framing and a color palette that feels product-specific instead of trend-following.

Example direction:

- Background: near-black or warm bone instead of plain white
- Headline font: Fraunces or Cormorant Garamond
- Body font: Geist or DM Sans
- Accent: rust, acid lime, cobalt, or oxblood instead of lavender-purple
- Buttons: squared or sharply rounded with strong contrast, minimal glow
- Cards: bordered, layered, and slightly offset instead of floating generic glass panels

In short: the design looks generic because it relies on defaults that already belong to a thousand other startups. The fix is not “make it prettier.” The fix is to choose a stronger visual thesis and make typography, color, layout, depth, and motion all reinforce that single point of view.
