Borrow tools for small household repairs. Kits are free for library members.

What is included

  • One screwdriver with interchangeable bits.
  • One adjustable wrench.
  • One tape measure.

Before you book

  • Bring your library card and photo identification.
  • Collect and return the kit at the service desk.
  • Keep small parts away from children.

Loan period

Borrow a kit for 7 days. Renew once if no one is waiting.

Collection hours

  • Monday–Friday: 09:00–18:00.
  • Saturday: 10:00–14:00.
  • Sunday: closed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Members aged 18 or over can borrow one kit at a time.

Availability

2 kits available at Central Branch.
