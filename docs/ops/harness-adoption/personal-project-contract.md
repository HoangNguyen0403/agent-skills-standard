Unknown cost, usage, consultation, or coverage is never zero, savings, or success. Preserve failed, retried, blocked, and not-started attempts in the denominator.

## Review and lifecycle

The implementer records evidence but cannot self-approve. Settled owner documents require a separate eligible reviewer to inspect the exact revision against every AC, the revision-bound brief, and quality. The owner reports independent acceptance as pending until that review occurs. A material repair invalidates affected evidence and requires re-exercise and fresh independent review. A PASS from the checklist applies only to the approved card at the reviewed revision; it is not G2/G3 approval or permission to merge, release, or deploy.
