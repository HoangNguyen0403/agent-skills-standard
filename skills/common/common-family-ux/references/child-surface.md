# Child Surface (ages 3-6)

- Pre-readers: every instruction is an icon plus a voice prompt; text is secondary.
- Targets 64px minimum, 16px apart; no drag longer than a short thumb move; no hidden gestures (long press, multi-finger, swipe-only).
- One task per screen; one obvious next step; back is always visible.
- Rewards are sticker or short animation moments, never points, streaks, leaderboards, or loss.
- No timers, no ads, no external links, no purchases, no chat. Anything leaving the child space sits behind a parent gate.
- Sessions end with a calm hand-back screen to the parent.
- Sound is optional and never the only signal; a visible mute control is always present.
- Tablet is the primary child device: landscape layout, co-viewing with a parent.
