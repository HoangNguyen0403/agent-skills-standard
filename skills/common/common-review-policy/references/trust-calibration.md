# Trust Calibration

This reference records evidence requirements for a possible future, separately approved contract change. Report-only is ALWAYS DISABLED in this implementation, even with complete M3 evidence, owner-set thresholds, and a purported human grant; this text creates no runtime conditional.

## Independent evidence

- Keep the independent seed cohort fixed before results are observed. Seed assignments are separate from accepted, merged, or deployed changes; measure recall against independently checked expected Blocker/Major defects, including misses.
- Use spot audits sampled from agent-reviewed revisions, not only accepted or merged work. Bind each finding to the exact reviewed revision and class; count unmerged and never-accepted revisions where applicable.
- Preserve `unknown` class/configuration and incomplete coverage as visible gaps. Missing populations, missing seed results, empty denominators, or incomplete coverage make a rate unavailable, never zero or a pass.
- Report numerator, denominator, sample size, coverage, source, cohort, observation horizon, and limitations. Do not infer quality from structural checks, self-reported declarations, or unsupported savings claims.
- The policy owner sets thresholds from the evidence and records the basis in the policy. This reference defines no target, sample size, acceptable rate, or automatic graduation rule.

## Post-grant escape

An observed post-grant escape requires suspension of that grant pending owner review, even when aggregate coverage is incomplete. Suspension does not lower the default human review or any stronger trunk, sensitive, or one-way line-review requirement. The agent reports evidence; only the owner decides whether a grant resumes, changes, or is revoked. No agent authenticates that decision or edits policy automatically.
