### Conceptual Primary References
1. **Scrum Guide (Ken Schwaber & Jeff Sutherland)**:
   - Reference: [Scrum Guide - Product Owner](https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html#product-owner)
   - Concept: Product Owner accountability for maximizing the value of the product resulting from the work of the Scrum Team.
2. **Evidence-Based Management (EBM, Scrum.org)**:
   - Reference: [EBM Guide](https://www.scrum.org/resources/online-evidence-based-management-guide)
   - Concept: Key Value Areas including Current Value and Unrealized Value; incremental measurement of value rather than activity output.
3. **SVPG Four Big Risks (Marty Cagan)**:
   - Reference: [The Four Big Risks](https://www.svpg.com/four-big-risks/)
   - Concept: Separation of financial viability risk from value, usability, and feasibility risks.
