## 2. Canonical Pinned Conceptual Sources (Frozen Register)

The following references represent canonical conceptual sources cited from the frozen repository register:

1. **Scrum Guide (2020)**:
   - Authors: Ken Schwaber and Jeff Sutherland
   - Canonical Link: [Scrum Guide Product Owner](https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html#product-owner)
   - Conceptual Influence: The Product Owner's accountability for ordering the Product Backlog to maximize value, anchored by a singular Product Goal.

2. **Evidence-Based Management (EBM)**:
   - Organization: Scrum.org
   - Canonical Link: [Scrum.org EBM Guide](https://www.scrum.org/resources/online-evidence-based-management-guide)
   - Conceptual Influence: Iterative goal-setting (Strategic Goal, Intermediate Goals, Immediate Tactical Goals) validated by empirical measurements rather than static Gantt calendars.

3. **Silicon Valley Product Group (SVPG)**:
   - References: Marty Cagan (*Inspired*, *Transformed*)
   - Canonical Link: [SVPG Four Big Risks](https://www.svpg.com/four-big-risks/)
   - Conceptual Influence: The transition from output-driven feature roadmaps to outcome-based roadmaps structured around problem opportunities; managing risk before commitment.

## 3. Supplemental Conceptual Reading

The following works provided high-level conceptual background regarding strategic focus and trade-offs, without providing text, code, or templates. They are supplemental reading and not pinned file inputs:

1. **Good Strategy / Bad Strategy**:
   - Author: Richard Rumelt (Crown Business, 2011)
   - Conceptual Influence: Strategy as focusing finite capacity against a pivotal challenge; the essential discipline that true strategy requires saying "no", making hard choices, and defining explicit non-goals.
