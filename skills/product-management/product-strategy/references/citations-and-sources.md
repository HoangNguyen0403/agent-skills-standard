## 2. Conceptual Citations

The following external works provided conceptual background for strategic reasoning and risk analysis, without providing text or templates:

1. **Scrum Guide (2020)**:
   - Authors: Ken Schwaber and Jeff Sutherland
   - Conceptual Influence: Defining the Product Owner as accountable for maximizing product value and managing the Product Goal as a clear long-term objective.

2. **Silicon Valley Product Group (SVPG)**:
   - References: Marty Cagan (*Inspired*, *Transformed*)
   - Conceptual Influence: Evaluating Value, Usability, Feasibility, and Business Viability risks before committing to delivery; separating opportunity assessment from solution delivery.

3. **Evidence-Based Management (EBM)**:
   - Organization: Scrum.org
   - Conceptual Influence: Aligning strategic goals with empirical evidence, intermediate goals, and measurable value metrics, avoiding unvalidated assumptions.

4. **Good Strategy / Bad Strategy**:
   - Author: Richard Rumelt
   - Conceptual Influence: The "Kernel of Strategy" (Diagnosis of the core challenge, a Guiding Policy for dealing with the challenge, and Coherent Actions designed to carry out the guiding policy), and the fundamental discipline of explicit non-goals and trade-offs.
