| Advisory | Patched 1.x | Patched 2.x | Patched 5.x |

**Fix:** Use advisory-patched, major-compatible scoped backports for minimatch3/minimatch9 (at least `1.1.21`/`2.1.7` for these three advisories), preserving `5.0.12` for minimatch10. Regenerate the lockfile and have the integration owner verify compatibility and the affected non-production paths; do not force 5.x onto old consumers or suppress advisories.
